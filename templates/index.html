Toolbox

Garden of Eden

Garden of Eden is a new (and very much work-in-progress) hatchery that aims to be mobile-friendly, secure and accessible.

  • Authorisation: Unlike most hatcheries, the Garden ensures nobody but you can add your dragons as the only way to add them is by logging in with Dragon Cave.
  • Modern: The most recent hatchery launched over a decade ago. There's been tons of changes online since then, and Garden of Eden takes advantage of that.
  • Mobile-friendly and accessible.
  • So many settings!
  • Cute illustrations of mints everywhere.
  • Choose from a lovely mint green or hide in the dark with a comfortable dark mode.
  • Compete for clicks in weekly rankings to earn yourself 1 of 10 coloured mint badges.
  • A dynamic scroll stat signature banner.
  • Trade your clicks for flairs in Matthias' shop: Display little flowers alongside your username.

Lineage Builder

Lineage builder allows users to preview dragon lineages.

  • Replicates how lineages would display on DC.
  • Clone and paste entire ancestors, switch parent genders, remove/add ancestors and descendants.
  • Codes and names are validated to ensure they are legal on DC (but will not be checked for availability.) A game changer for you lyrical lineage builders out there.
  • Share public links to your created lineages. Example! You can even import them into the editor.
  • Partial support for time-based sprites and spriter alts (where permission has been granted).
  • Restrict the breeds shown by using the filters.
  • Preview how your lineage appears on different skins such as default, portal 2 and mobile dark.
  • Insert your own lineage tiles and preview them as if they were on DC, while never uploading them publicly.
  • Create checker lineages easily using the Checker Generator.
  • Change multiple tiles at once using the mass-selection tools.
  • Mobile-friendly.
  • Check your dragons for inbreeding using the inbred checker.
  • Odeen approves of it, which should say it all really.

Auto-refresher (FART)

Fast Auto Refreshing Tool, aka FART (yes, really!). Fart is an auto-refreshing tool.

  • Refresh multiple dragons in a simple, control-panel style layout.
  • Control various things, such as refresh speed and views per refresh.
  • Mobile-friendly.
  • State persistence. Your settings won't be lost even if you refresh.
  • Estimated views per minute.
  • Leave FART on in the background and Smart Removal will remove dragons as they hatch or grow up.
  • Prepare in advance by creating a share link with the dragons and exact settings to come back to later.
  • Input the dragon's tick down minutes and seconds, and FART will calculate the rest of the TOD by giving you a countdown and your local time.

Calendar

An experimental calendar that shows breed availabilities in times local to you. Times are fully localised to your browser language and you can pick the timezone you want them shown in.

  • All dates and times will be shown in the format of your browser.
  • Choose your own timezone or a different one.
  • Breeds display a helpful tooltip indicating what biome they'll be available in.
